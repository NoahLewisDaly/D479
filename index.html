Know before you go

Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros, yen and major credit cards.

Getting around

Public buses in Taniti City run 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. every day. Taxis, rental cars and bikes are available; bike helmets are required by law.

Holiday closures

Many attractions and restaurants close on Taniti's national holidays, so check dates before you plan.

Alcohol & age

The drinking age is 18. Alcohol is not sold or served from midnight to 9 a.m.

Where to stay

All lodging on Taniti is regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
